LANTERN — one gun / two guns, and every power-up state drawn ON THE SHIP

The power-ups RUN here, like in the game. Space fires the ship's real shot cadence (0.5 s, or 0.38 s with quick shot) at the ±14 px double offsets or on the centreline for single; L fires the laser beam on a 0.8 s gap; Q launches bombs on a 1.1 s gap. R quick shot, Z time warp and B bomb keep running while selected (buttons run them too). G runs the single ↔ two-gun animation. The ship keeps the same soft alpha as the other mockups. POWER-UP STATES (KEYS L / R / Z / B)

What each state is. These are the four live game power-ups, drawn on LANTERN's own geometry instead of only in the HUD text:

LASER — two cyan emitter pods on the nose shoulders flare, and a lens ring at the bow swells open while a beam is firing (js/player.js:157 laserBeams).
QUICK SHOT — an amber halo ring over the gun housing pulses; this is the rapid-fire effect (effects.rapid), the wait between shots cut to 0.76×.
TIME WARP — two violet rings around the hull spin and breathe (effects.slow), so the hull visibly sits inside the slowed bubble.
BOMB — a launch flare cone off the bow while the bomb is airborne, plus a charge bar on the spine that fills when the bomb is ready.

Same kit, same width. Everything here goes through js/ships3d.js and the game's own bundle. Every state part sits inside x ±36 and is authored at alpha 0, so LANTERN still spans exactly 72 model units / 75.6 px.
