WAVE 1 — the game frame, with the squid and the crab in the new look

Everything is the real wave 1: 3 rows x 11 (rows 1-2 squid with armor on the top row, row 3 crab), 4 bunkers, player, ground line, HUD, march and descend — copied from the game's own numbers. Only the two mob types are swappable: SQUID: 3D / CRAB: 3D put that mob on the transparent WebGL layer above the 2D canvas, in the same spot, same 32px size, marching with the grid. Turn a chip off and that mob is drawn by the game's own neon() silhouette again, byte-for-byte the current look.
The 3D mobs use the in-game colors (squid #99d4ff, crab #ffd26b) and the same shader as squid-3d.html: toon ramp with saturated shadow, sky/back rim, and a thin hue-tinted hull ink (~1px) — that is the ink that fits at 32px. INK BLACK (EVEN) is the even, screen-space ink from squid-3d.html: it is built from depth edges, and at this size (a 32px mob with 6 legs, 2 eyes and a mantle — measured: ~80% of the mob's pixels are depth steps) it floods the whole sprite. It is left off by default and is on the chip so you can see exactly what that means; use it on the big mobs in squid-3d.html. Bunkers, player, armor dome, ground, HUD and the flat neon mobs stay canvas-2D, exactly as shipped. TURN (SHOW 3D) rocks each mob so you can see the volume.
