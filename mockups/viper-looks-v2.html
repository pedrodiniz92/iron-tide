WINGED VIPER — SHIPPED vs VENOM WYRM

Key 1 = the shipped viper (default), key 2 = VENOM WYRM. Both models come from the game's own builder map through the bundled js/assets3d.bundle.js: keys viper and viper_wyrm (js/viper3d.js) - no copies in this page. The real game draws the same builders when loaded as index.html?viper=1; without the parameter it stays on the shipped viper.
1 SHIPPED (default)flat neon dart, no face. The baseline.
2 VENOM WYRM (?viper=1)spread cobra hood, slit-pupil eyes under brow ridges, fangs.
